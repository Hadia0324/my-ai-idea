# My AI Idea

## Summary
My AI idea is to make a friendly AI helper that talks to people in simple and easy language. Many students feel confused when they try to learn new topics because books and websites use difficult words. My AI will help them by answering their questions in simple words, giving them examples, and helping them make a study plan. It will be like a kind teacher who is always available to help. My goal is to make learning easy, fun, and friendly for everyone, especially for students who are just starting to learn about AI. I want my AI to listen carefully, explain clearly, and encourage students to keep learning and asking questions.

## How it works
My project has a Python file called hello-ai.py. When you run it, it asks for your name and your AI idea. Then it talks to you and shows your idea back. It is a simple first step to show how AI can have a conversation.
